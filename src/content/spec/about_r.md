# About me

I am **Yufan Wei**, a second-year Ph.D. student at <img src="/logos/ucsd.svg" alt="UCSD" class="org-logo org-logo-inline" />[University of California, San Diego](https://www.ece.ucsd.edu/), advised by [Prof. Biwei Huang](https://biweihuang.com/) and working closely with [Dr. Kun Zhou](https://lancelot39.github.io/). My research focuses on causal intelligence and Embodied AI, with a particular interest in representation-level modeling across policy models and world models. Previously, I obtained my B.S. in Computer Science from the <img src="/logos/umn.svg" alt="UMN" class="org-logo org-logo-inline" />University of Minnesota.
